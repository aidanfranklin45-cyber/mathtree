Glossary: Understanding Your Deal Metrics

⚡ Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

The annualized rate of return that equates the present value of all cash flows (including net sale equity at exit) to your initial investment. Think of it as the speed at which your capital grows. A higher IRR is better, with institutional real estate typically targeting 12% to 18%.

💸 Net Present Value (NPV)

The current value of all future cash flows (cash flow + exit proceeds), discounted back to today using a target discount rate (hurdle rate), minus your initial cash investment.

Equity Multiplier (MOIC)

How many times your cash investment is returned over the hold period. Calculated as Total Cash Returned / Total Cash Invested.
