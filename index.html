Glossary: Understanding Your Deal Metrics

📊 Capitalization Rate (Cap Rate)

The rate of return on an investment property based on its income. Calculated as $\text{NOI} / \text{Property Value}$.
• Entry Cap Rate: Year 1 return based on the initial purchase price.
• Target (Exit) Cap Rate: The expected yield rate when selling the asset at exit.
• Gradual Cap Rate Interpolation: Rather than a sudden Year 2 value jump, our model decompress/compresses the cap rate linearly from the Entry Cap Rate (Year 1) to the Target Cap Rate (Exit Year) over the hold period, creating a smooth, realistic Year-over-Year property valuation.

⏱️ Internal Rate of Return (IRR)

The annualized rate of return that equates the present value of all cash flows (including net sale equity at exit) to your initial investment. Think of it as the speed at which your capital grows. A higher IRR is better, with institutional real estate typically targeting 12% to 18%.

💸 Net Present Value (NPV)

The current value of all future cash flows (cash flow + exit proceeds), discounted back to today using a target discount rate (hurdle rate), minus your initial cash investment.
• NPV > $0: The deal meets and exceeds your target hurdle rate (profitable).
• NPV < $0: The deal does not meet your target rate (underperforming).

📈 Equity Multiplier (MOIC)

How many times your cash investment is returned over the hold period. Calculated as $\text{Total Cash Returned} / \text{Total Cash Invested}$. For example, a 2.0x multiplier means you doubled your money (e.g., invested $100k, received $200k total).
