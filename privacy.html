DATA GOVERNANCE & TENANT ISOLATION

Institutional Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 2026 • Version: 2.6 Enterprise • Data Controller: MathTree Inc.
OUR CORE INSTITUTIONAL PRIVACY COMMITMENT

MathTree recognizes the extreme proprietary sensitivity of commercial real estate pipelines, acquisition bids, in-place rent rolls, tenant identities, lease structures, and investor capital splits. MathTree does not sell, broker, or syndicate deal parameters, nor do we train public AI foundational models on your private underwriting files.

1. Information We Collect

We collect and process the following categories of information to provide institutional underwriting and modeling services:

  • Account & Identity Credentials: User email addresses, authentication identifiers, full names, corporate organizational affiliations, and primary entity linkages.
  • Project & Underwriting Datasets: Property addresses, county parcel numbers, purchase prices, capitalization rates, square footage, unit counts, unit-mix rental schedules, debt financing parameters, expense ratios, and waterfall structures.
  • Transactional Billing Records: Subscription tier, billing cycle records, invoice receipts, and tokenized payment identifiers handled securely through payment processing partners (including Stripe). We never store raw credit card numbers or bank credentials on MathTree servers.
  • System Telemetry & Diagnostics: IP addresses, browser user-agents, calculation latency metrics, and error logs required for platform security and performance optimization.

2. How We Utilize Collected Information

Your information is utilized strictly to:

  • Execute 10-year pro-formas, debt amortizations, and stochastic Monte Carlo risk calculations.
  • Generate downloadable executive pitch decks and investment committee briefs.
  • Authenticate your user session and maintain enterprise Row-Level Security (RLS) policies.
  • Maintain audit trails and enforce automated session timeouts (e.g. 30-minute inactivity thresholds).
  • Provide customer support, billing administration, and platform maintenance notices.

3. Data Isolation & Architectural Security Controls

We implement rigorous administrative, physical, and technical safeguards engineered to protect institutional assets:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between client terminals, API gateways, and database nodes are encrypted via TLS 1.3.
  • Encryption at Rest: Database records, storage snapshots, and authentication secrets are encrypted using AES-256 standard encryption.
  • Multi-Tenant Row-Level Security (RLS): Projects, financial models, and profile data are protected by cryptographic database row-level security policies, ensuring no user can query or modify data belonging to another enterprise or account.
  • Inactivity Session Timeouts: Unattended terminals are automatically cleared after 30 minutes of idle time to prevent physical terminal compromise.

4. Third-Party Sub-Processors

We engage select enterprise infrastructure partners strictly to fulfill cloud computing and authentication obligations:

  • Supabase (PostgreSQL & Auth): Secure database hosting, session token verification, and RLS enforcement.
  • Google Cloud / Firebase Hosting: Content distribution, static edge hosting, and TLS certificate termination.
  • Stripe: PCI-DSS Level 1 certified subscription payment orchestration.

Each infrastructure partner is bound by strict Data Processing Agreements (DPAs) requiring enterprise-grade security controls.

5. California Privacy Disclosures (CalOPPA, CCPA & CPRA)

Pursuant to the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents enjoy specific privacy entitlements:

  • Right to Know & Access: You may request disclosure of the specific pieces and categories of personal information collected.
  • Right to Delete: You have the right to request deletion of your personal data and underwritten project records, subject to statutory exceptions.
  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information: MathTree does not sell or share personal information or commercial deal records for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Do Not Track: We do not track users across third-party websites or online services.

6. European Union & UK Disclosures (GDPR & UK-GDPR)

For users located within the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, MathTree processes data based on: (a) performance of our contract with you; (b) compliance with statutory obligations; and (c) legitimate commercial interests in securing and maintaining platform reliability.

You possess rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability, and the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

7. Data Retention & Permanent Erasure

Underwritten deals, pro-forma models, and associated financial records remain stored within your account tenancy until deleted by your authorized administrators. Upon explicit account closure or written erasure request, all associated proprietary records will be permanently purged in accordance with standard enterprise data deletion cycles.

8. Data Protection Officer & Inquiries

For questions regarding data processing practices, privacy rights requests, or institutional security architecture verification, contact our Data Governance team at privacy@mathtree.io.
